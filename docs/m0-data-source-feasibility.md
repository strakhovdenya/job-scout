**Status:** Accepted as a completed feasibility investigation by owner direction on 2026-11-27; source activation is not approved.

**Owner disposition (2026-11-27):** Proceed to a narrowly scoped local ingestion PRD, with Jobicy first and Himalayas conditional on its relevant usage, retention and processing requirements. Aggregator application destinations are acceptable. Ukrainian-employer coverage is a separate metric, not a first-pilot blocker. JobScout remains private with a public portfolio repository; no real vacancy datasets or recruiter contacts belong in Git. The owner subsequently approved [M2 PRD revision 1](m2-job-ingestion-prd.md) for technical planning, deferred until a separate engineering workflow improvement task is completed. Acceptance does not establish reliable market coverage, activate any source or authorize execution of the 14-day pilot. The investigation and its original pending-review language below are preserved as the historical record; this disposition supersedes those pending product decisions.

**Verification date:** 2026-11-27 (Europe/Warsaw). HTTP measurements below were made on that date, principally at 21:36–21:37 UTC. This is a point-in-time investigation, not a longitudinal availability study.

## Problem and value

### Executive summary

JobScout needs a repeatable, permitted supply of vacancies for a Germany-based TypeScript/Node.js backend or full-stack developer, including applied AI work. German remote employers, Ukrainian employers accepting people abroad, and international employers accepting Germany-based workers are the target. Candidate language proficiency remains unspecified.

**Recommendation: conditional Go for a narrowly scoped, owner-approved ingestion pilot; No-Go for claiming that reliable target-market coverage has already been demonstrated.** Public API access works, descriptions are available, and promising technical records exist. Sustainable weekly yield, German employment eligibility, Ukrainian remote technical coverage, and permission for several intended uses remain unproven.

Recommend **Jobicy free API as the first MVP candidate**: its published API rules expressly cover storage, retention and AI analysis. Accept that its free application destination is a Jobicy page. **Himalayas is a useful second candidate**, with strong country/timezone data, subject to resolving retention of derived results and the JobFlow export restriction. Company-specific ATS feeds can improve precision after permission and company onboarding checks. Arbeitnow needs permission clarification; DOU and Remotive are excluded from the initial pilot pending conflicting terms being resolved.

The measured first-page samples contained 300 Arbeitnow records, 50 filtered Jobicy records, 27 N26 records, 77 n8n records, 11 Personio-feed records, 25 Ajax records and 9 Readdle records. An additional permitted source, Himalayas, returned 20 filtered records. These are **records, not unique relevant jobs**. No cross-source deduplication, suitability assessment or contractual eligibility verification was performed.

### Observable value

A candidate should receive fresh opportunities with source provenance, readable descriptions, truthful application destinations and explicit uncertainty. Volume alone is insufficient: a remote sales role in Germany or a Kyiv office development role does not meet the target. Unknown language or eligibility must remain reviewable rather than being silently rejected.

### Evidence conventions and experiment protocol

- **D (documented):** stated in the linked official documentation or terms. **E (experimental):** observed in a live response. **U (unverified):** not established, including silence in terms. A successful GET is not a reuse licence.
- All external references in this document were checked on **2026-11-27**. Provider publication/update dates can differ. Search results were used to locate primary pages; third-party scraper claims were not treated as documentation or permission.
- Manual, bounded HTTPS GETs only, without credentials, cookies, browser automation or LLM APIs. PowerShell `Invoke-WebRequest`, generic `User-Agent: JobScout-M0-Feasibility/1.0`, 25-second timeout, no automatic redirects, no retries. Requests were sequential. Documentation redirects were handled by the web research tool separately.
- Initial sandbox networking failed before an HTTP response; the authorized public requests succeeded through the network-enabled execution path. This is not provider downtime. No 429, CAPTCHA or access restriction was bypassed.
- Live data stayed in process memory. Only aggregate measurements and field names are recorded here; no response files, vacancy dataset, personal names, email addresses or recruiter information were added to Git.
- Small explicit batches bounded the experiment; no automatic traversal or polling ran. Repeated reads for aggregate analysis are not independent samples.

**Request accounting:** Arbeitnow 2; Jobicy 5; Greenhouse 3 (N26 twice, Readdle once); Lever 3 (empty documentation board, invalid token, Ajax); Ashby 2; Personio 2; Himalayas 3. Total **20** network-enabled GETs, with **19 HTTP 200 and one HTTP 404**. The initial connection failure is additional. Web documentation retrievals and failed RSS rendering are separate.

### Practical vacancy coverage

This is a convenience sample with **different selection mechanisms**: latest unfiltered Arbeitnow page; filtered Jobicy engineering page; purposively chosen ATS boards; one translated Personio feed; first Ajax page; country/keyword-filtered Himalayas page. It cannot rank total market size or establish statistical coverage.

Measurements used in-memory title/text checks after two HTML-decode passes and tag removal:

- **T:** title matches case-insensitive `back.?end|full.?stack|software (engineer|developer)|node\.?js|typescript|applied ai|ai engineer|machine learning engineer|product engineer`.
- **TS:** T plus description match `typescript|node\.?js|nestjs`. This is a retrieval heuristic, not required-skill extraction; it misses other useful titles and can include irrelevant ones.
- **Description:** more than 100 characters in the returned description field/blocks. This measures substantial text, not independent proof that the original vacancy is complete.
- **Location:** nonempty source location/office/eligibility text; country mentions are lexical hints, not employer nationality or contractual eligibility. DE-hint pattern: `germany|deutschland|berlin|munich|münchen|hamburg|^DE$`; UA-hint: `ukrain|kyiv|kiev|lviv`. For Ashby, primary and secondary location names were combined; for Himalayas, restriction arrays were combined.
- **Language marker:** English/German/Deutsch/Englisch within 70 characters, without a period/newline, of `fluent|fluency|proficien|B[12]|C[12]|kenntnis|flie.end|verhandlungssicher|communication|command`, or the reverse order for the fluency/proficiency/German subsets. Counts locate possible explicit requirements; they are **not validated required/preferred/proficiency findings**. They may miss requirements or match unrelated prose.
- **Email marker:** address-shaped text in returned description only. Context must establish application purpose; recruiter identity was not harvested. General, privacy and accommodation contacts are not application addresses.

**Employer coverage:** the Arbeitnow sample contains 142 distinct employer-name strings and Jobicy 21; Himalayas contains 17. These are not verified legal entities or country counts. **Usefulness assessment:** Himalayas gave the strongest immediate TS-text yield in a deliberately filtered sample (18/20); Jobicy offered four TS-text candidates in 50 engineering listings; Arbeitnow six in an unfiltered 300. These are not comparable recall estimates. N26's zero T titles does not imply no technical hiring overall. Ajax's first page is dominated by other functions and cannot assess its technical board. None of these numbers establishes the weekly number of newly relevant, unique, Germany-eligible jobs.

### Risks, limitations, security and cost

Permission ambiguity is the principal activation blocker for most ATS/board sources. Record provider terms, employer-specific scope, verification date and revocation/removal procedure before ingestion. Public portfolio operation may differ from personal noncommercial use. JobFlow export is a separate use, not an automatic consequence of storage permission.

Sampling bias and cached data limit conclusions. Arbeitnow's observed cache conflicts with its hourly metadata; Himalayas' `pubDate` semantics differ from its name; Jobicy's two official page-size limits conflict. No historical uptime, response-schema drift, deletion latency, weekly novelty rate or cross-source duplicate rate was measured. Pagination was actually continued only for Jobicy. Publication/last-update field presence does not prove freshness or first-publication semantics.

“Germany”, “Europe”, “remote”, employer headquarters, work authorization, contracting options and payroll/EOR coverage are separate facts. Do not infer German contractual eligibility from any of the first four. Language and employment unknowns must remain unknown. Do not use narrow keyword heuristics as automatic skip rules.

Future adapters must bound pages, bytes, runtime and retries; allowlist HTTPS hosts and reject unexpected redirects; avoid following description links; validate individual records; sanitize untrusted HTML; disable XML external entities; preserve provenance and source-specific lifecycle evidence. Content is untrusted, including possible prompt injection. Store contacts only where necessary and explicitly identified, under an approved retention policy. Never commit real samples or contact details.

This study used no paid source, AI call or cloud resource. A future pilot can use the local Worker and Emulator; no new framework, workspace package, database, cloud deployment or ADR is justified by these findings alone. Runtime, storage, retention and future model processing still require explicit budgets; a free endpoint is not a guarantee of free operation or availability. Suggested pilot ceiling: two scheduled passes/day, at most two pages and 100 records per source/pass, one request at a time, at most one bounded retry honoring Retry-After. These are proposed limits for owner review, not implemented settings. Large whole-board ATS responses need an independent response-byte limit and completeness check before closure inference.
