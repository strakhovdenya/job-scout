# ADR-006: Conservative preliminary screening

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The candidate wants likely opportunities surfaced and already has a detailed matching system.

## Alternatives considered

A ranking model or detailed eligibility prediction hides uncertainty and duplicates JobFlow.

## Decision

Use worth_exploring, maybe and skip for preliminary triage, prioritize recall and require textual evidence for skip. Human selection controls further analysis.

## Consequences

Text matches alone cannot establish semantic entailment. Preserve uncertainties and defer evaluation, complete heuristics and export to their milestones.
